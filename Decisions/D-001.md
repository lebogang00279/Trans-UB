# D-001 — Offline Payment and Physical Handover

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

TRANS-UB will **not** process online payments. Payment and physical handover of approved low-risk physical goods will be arranged directly between buyer and seller outside the platform.

TRANS-UB records the Order, its lifecycle state and the buyer's confirmation of receipt. It does not handle, store, verify or transfer money and does not provide delivery/logistics services.

## Rationale

Online payment integration would add technical complexity, security obligations, external-provider dependencies, regulatory considerations and potential financial liability that are outside the approved semester-project scope.

Students already use cash and mobile-money methods independently. Keeping payment outside the platform allows TRANS-UB to focus on discovery, reservation, transaction recording, moderation and reputation.

Physical handover is also outside the platform. TRANS-UB may recommend public campus handover locations, but the buyer and seller arrange the actual meeting.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Full online payment integration | Rejected | Adds external integration, financial-security obligations and liability. |
| Mobile-money integration | Rejected | Requires provider-specific integration and payment/fraud handling. |
| Escrow | Rejected | Requires TRANS-UB to hold/release money and assume additional financial/legal responsibility. |
| Offline payment and handover | **Selected** | Keeps Phase 1 focused on the approved marketplace workflow. |

## Consequences

- TRANS-UB cannot independently prove that money changed hands.
- Buyer and seller remain responsible for the offline exchange.
- The system provides accountability through transaction states:
  1. Order is Pending.
  2. Seller accepts.
  3. Offline handover occurs.
  4. Seller marks Delivered.
  5. Buyer confirms receipt.
  6. Order becomes Completed.
- Only Completed orders may unlock reviews.
- Order/status history may support later report handling.
- User-facing text must not imply that TRANS-UB guarantees payment or handover safety.
