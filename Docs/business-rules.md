                                            Business Rules & Variants
                                            
•	Student Identity Check: Accounts cannot be created unless the user's student email is verified against the simulated student database.

•	Order Lifecycle Sequence: An order cannot be set to "Completed" until it has first gone through the "Delivered" stage.

•	Feedback Restrictions: Buyers can only leave a review if it is tied to a "Completed" order, and each order is strictly limited to one review.

•	Zero Direct Payments: TRANS UB never handles, processes, or holds money. All payments and item exchanges take place directly between students off the platform.

•	Reservation Window: An item is not officially reserved for a buyer until the seller approves the request. Up until that point, other users can still ask to buy it.

•	Stock Lockout: The second an item is marked as sold, any other pending order requests for that exact listing are automatically blocked.
