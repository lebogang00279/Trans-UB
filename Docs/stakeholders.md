# TRANS-UB — Stakeholders

*Extracted from [Docs/project-proposal-and-approval.md](project-proposal-and-approval.md) (CSI473 Project Problem Proposal and Approval Form, submitted 10 August 2026) for standalone reference.*

| Stakeholder/role | Need | Concern | Evidence/access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student business owner (seller) | To reach fellow students easily and manage their own listings | Being trusted by new customers, and losing visibility when posts get buried | Interviews with five student sellers; two are willing to test the system |
| Student customer (buyer) | To find and compare products and services quickly | Being scammed or ignored by a seller they cannot verify | Interviews with five student buyers; WhatsApp group screenshots |
| SRC (student representative body) | Oversight of trading activity involving students | Fraud between students, prohibited goods and disputes with no record | SRC office is reachable on campus; not yet formally consulted |
| University management | Student safety and protection of the university name | Liability if something goes wrong in trading linked to campus | Indirect stakeholder, treated through our policy and moderation rules |
| Platform administrator (our team, then a student moderator) | Tools to review listings and act on reports | Handling reports fairly and keeping banned items off the platform | Role designed and operated by the project team |

## Notes

- **Direct system users:** Student Buyer, Student Seller, Platform administrator — these three interact with the system directly (see [Models/system-context.md](../Models/system-context.md) for their data flows).
- **Indirect/oversight stakeholders:** SRC and University Management do not use the system day to day but have an oversight, escalation, or liability interest in it.
- **Not yet consulted:** the SRC has not been formally consulted at the time of writing; this is a known gap to close before or during the semester.
